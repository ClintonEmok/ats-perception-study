<objective>
Replace the 24 single-timeline ATS perception trials with 12 A/B comparison trials driven by the showcase analysis.

Purpose: The showcase analysis selected 6 windows; expanding to 12 windows (3 per duration) and replacing the within-trial single-condition presentation with a side-by-side A/B comparison halves participant burden and produces a more diagnostic cross-task, cross-duration signal.

Output:
- Regenerated `data.json` with 12 windows (1d#{1,3,5}, 14d#{1,3,5}, 30d#{1,3,5}, 90d#{1,3,5}) and 6 pre-baked A/B orderings
- New experiment config (slug `ats-perception-v5`), 3-cycle task latin square, 6-ordering A/B rotation
- New `ConvexWrites` signature and Convex schema for `studyResponses` (choice A/B, rationale, responseTimeMs, confidence, windowKey, taskType)
- New `ABComparisonScreen` component replacing the deleted single-timeline `TrialRunner`
- Manual "Next" navigation (no auto-advance)
- `/study/exp2-interactive` moderator page untouched
</objective>
